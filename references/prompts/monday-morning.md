# Monday Morning (runs after Phase 8 — post-hackathon)

Prepare the team for what happens AFTER the hackathon.
Read all artefacts. EDTH's mission: "Put you in a position to continue
towards deployment." Bridge demo day to deployment.

---

## STEP 1 — Fundraising & grants

Identify 3-5 specific funding mechanisms relevant to this project:

Search: "[solution domain] EU defence innovation funding 2026"
Search: "[solution domain] EDF EDIP EUDIS grant 2026"
Search: "[solution domain] NATO DIANA accelerator"

For each, answer: what is it, who's eligible, typical grant size, deadline
(if known), application difficulty (low/medium/high).

European-specific: EDF (European Defence Fund), EDIP, EUDIS (EU Defence
Innovation Scheme), HEDI (Hub for EU Defence Innovation), NATO DIANA,
national MoD innovation arms. Do NOT list US programs (SBIR, OTA) unless
the team explicitly wants US market entry.

---

## STEP 2 — Key contacts

Identify 3-5 organizations or individuals the team should talk to:

  - Defense primes active in this domain (Helsing, Quantum Systems, etc.)
  - Government / MoD innovation units relevant to this solution
  - Accelerators or incubators (Brave1 in Ukraine, H4 in UK, etc.)
  - Frontline units or testers who could evaluate a prototype

For each: name, why them, how to reach (LinkedIn, email if public,
their hackathon/scout program).

---

## STEP 3 — 30 / 60 / 90 day plan

Produce a concrete timeline:

**Next 30 days:**
  - 3-5 concrete actions the team can take now
  - Example: "Polish the demo into a standalone repo."
  - Example: "Schedule a 15-min call with [contact name]."
  - Example: "Apply to [EDF/EUDIS grant] — deadline is [date]."

**Next 60 days:**
  - 2-3 actions for the medium term
  - Example: "Submit to NATO DIANA accelerator."
  - Example: "Field-test with [unit name] — contact via [name]."

**Next 90 days:**
  - 1-2 actions for the long term
  - Example: "Incorporate and raise pre-seed."
  - Example: "Apply for EDIP production scaling funding."

---

## STEP 4 — One-paragraph pitch for external audiences

Write a 3-4 sentence pitch suitable for a cold email to a defense
investor or MoD innovation unit. No jargon. Lead with the problem,
then the solution, then the ask.

Example: "At EDTH Munich 2025, we built [X] — a [one-liner]. It solves
[operational pain] that currently costs [Y sorties / Z hours / €X per
countermeasure]. We're looking for [Z] to field-test this with an
operational unit. Can we schedule 15 minutes?"
